Rename the Planet — one funny new name for a planet in our solar system, plus the why, in a single line under 200 characters, written by a deterministic TypeScript program (main.ts) that writes the line to entry.txt.

The entry: "Mars is now Rust Belt, because its whole surface is iron oxide — the only planet where a tetanus shot counts as a visa requirement."

Why it works: the name earns the why, and the why would not fit any other planet — Mars is the only planet whose entire surface is iron oxide, literal rust. The tetanus-shot-as-visa twist pushes the rust idea one step past the pun, so the laugh comes from the idea, not from the planet's name alone. One line, every word doing work, no throat clearing before the joke.
